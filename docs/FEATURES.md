# Features

## Public experience

Three independently branded menus; corresponding generated food photographs; category filters; availability; descriptions/allergens; product extras and double-size options; persistent cart; server-priced checkout; public-session pickup slots and capacity; contact collection; collection payment and labeled demonstration payment; protected order tracking; automatic status refresh; upcoming public events; directions; public QR image; read-only offline menu snapshot; installable manifest/icons; printable order summary.

## Management

Owner overview with persisted metrics and daily chart; order queues/status filters and touch controls; product creation/editing and availability; categories and display order; ingredient/supplier records; stock restock/adjustment/waste, reservations and history; recipe editing; calendar day/week/month views; public/private events and weekly repeats; event-specific menu; interactive location selection and saved locations; optional foreground GPS sharing; customer aggregates, notes/history/anonymization; date/status/location/event/category-filtered sales, product and location reports; CSV export; staff creation; tasks; branding preview, primary/secondary/accent colors, light/dark appearance, public slug, social links, logo upload and versioned configuration import/export.

## Permissions

Owner: full business management. Manager: operational management, customers and reports. Staff: own-business operational dashboard, orders, availability, location view and eligible tasks. Unauthenticated visitors: public information and orders authorized by their tracking cookie. No global marketplace or cross-business administrative interface is provided.

## Deliberate limits

This is a single-server local/self-hosted application. GPS works only while the sharing page is open and permission remains granted; it is not background employee tracking. Maps require tile connectivity and a provider policy appropriate to the intended traffic. Geocoding/search is not configured; manual address/coordinate entry works. Public tracking is tied to the ordering browser. Automatic external email and real online payments require integration configuration.

The original long brief also describes broader future capabilities: a specialized bundle SKU editor, drag-and-drop category ordering (numeric order editing is provided), separate verified customer accounts/marketing consent management, historical availability-duration analytics. Configurable required/optional modifier groups and Lisbon DST-aware weekly schedules are implemented. This implementation provides the operational subset described above; these advanced capabilities are not claimed as delivered. Fiscal invoices and legal compliance certification are outside this application.
